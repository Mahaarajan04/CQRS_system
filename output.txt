  Direct: PostgreSQL 3NF INSERT                  412      45.5ms     73.9ms

  [92mCQRS write is 1.4x higher throughput than direct PG INSERT[0m

  [93mWhy CQRS write scales better at high load:[0m
    MongoDB append: no FK checks, no schema enforcement, pure sequential write.
    Direct PG INSERT: 3 round-trips (customers + orders + order_items),
    FK constraint validation, index updates on every INSERT.
    Under contention (many concurrent writers) PG lock waits dominate.
